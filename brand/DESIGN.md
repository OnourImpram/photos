# DESIGN.md — @kecipsikologu

## 1. Purpose
A clinically responsible Turkish-language psychology editorial identity: warm, intelligent, grounded, distinct. Content is psychoeducational and never implies a diagnosis from lifestyle or income.

## 2. Palette
| Token | Reference | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F4F0E7` | Primary off-white canvas |
| Ink Green | `#1E352D` | Main editorial headings |
| Sage | `#A0A996` | Secondary illustrations |
| Warm Copper | `#A17656` | Selective emphasis |
| Charcoal | `#262522` | Body copy |

These are reference colors; artwork should use nuanced shades, not flat full-page color blocks.

## 3. Typography
- High-contrast **editorial serif** for meaningful headlines; neutral, exceptionally legible sans-serif for paragraphs.
- Turkish orthography matters: İ/ı, Ğ/ğ, Ş/ş, Ö/ö, Ü/ü, Ç/ç; proofread all rendered text manually.
- Do not let an image generator decide the final copy or line breaks. Prefer a separate editable text layer over illustration source files.

## 4. Layout
- Instagram feed: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5)**.
- Keep generous gutters, a clear visual hierarchy, and space for the text to breathe.
- Usually one core idea, one visual motif per slide.
- Consistent upper-left imprint: **@kecipsikologu** (not “Keçi Psikoloğu”).
- Upper-right slide count `1/N`, `2/N`, etc., unless imagery requires a more readable placement.

## 5. Illustration language
- Original **2D ink-and-watercolor editorial drawing** with restrained paper texture and light tactile detail.
- Simple notebooks, annotations, branching paths, original line drawings, quiet architectural or outdoor motifs.
- No generated humans, pseudo-clinical photos, glossy CGI, fake client scenes, or sterile wellness stock photography.
- Only use objects with conceptual purpose. Drawn leaves, mugs and books must never become automatic filler.

## 6. Accessibility
- Legible on a mobile device without zoom; do not compress long explanations into decorative calligraphy.
- Provide accurate, concise Turkish alt text for each slide in Buffer.
- Maintain readable contrast and sequential logic; keep visuals and captions complementary.

## 7. Content integrity
- Clinical claims must match their evidence and limitations.
- Visual metaphors are not diagnoses or validated psychological assessments.
- Avoid unsupported biological quantities, therapeutic outcome promises, and pressure to seek services.
- Use personal stories only with approval and privacy safeguards.

## 8. Anti-patterns
- No gradients, glassmorphism, shiny 3D badges, generic icons, decorative clutter, fake handwriting on every element, or forced aphorisms.
- Do not create a false “good life versus bad life” binary; context and individual differences are central.
- The public brand should communicate thoughtfulness and reliability without status signaling or income targeting.

## 9. Production and publishing
- Final release image must be approved, 1080 × 1350 px, cleanly proofread and sequentially numbered.
- For scheduled posts, keep asset URLs valid and unchanged through successful publication.
- Use the GitHub `photos` repository as the public asset source; Buffer is the scheduler, Instagram the distribution channel.
